## Summary & Insights

1. Approved Signals Do NOT Guarantee Profit: While the majority of closed trades from approved signals were profitable (76.4% win rate on closed approved signals), there are still instances (4 trades) where an approved signal resulted in a loss.
2. Winning Trades Do NOT Require Approval: There are instances where trades resulted in a profit even though the underlying signal was not formally marked as APPROVED (4 winning trades).
3. Low Execution/Completion Rate: A significant portion of your approved signals (90 out of 107) are not contributing to the closed PnL yet. Investigating why these signals are not executing or closing might help improve overall trading frequency.
